THE STORY

A hobby I couldn’t put down.

For a long time, coffee was just coffee to me. Then I had a cup at a specialty café that tasted like nothing I’d had before — sweet, bright, almost fruity. I couldn’t stop thinking about it. How could coffee taste like that?

Not long after, I brought home my first espresso machine, a Breville Bambino Plus, determined to find out.

What began as a hobby quickly turned into something I wanted to understand properly. One shot at a time, I learned to dial in espresso, practised latte art, and discovered how origin, processing, roasting and growing conditions can completely change what you taste in the cup.

Today, my setup is built around a La Marzocco Linea Micra, and coffee is still one of the things I enjoy most. Most of my free time goes into making flat whites, experimenting with espresso, and searching for coffees where you can genuinely taste where they came from.

Eventually I realized I didn’t just want to drink great coffee. I wanted other people to taste what I’d found. My family encouraged me to take the leap, and Ember Elevation is the result: a hobby I love, turned into a way to share it with you.

Why “Ember Elevation”? “Ember” is the heat and craft behind roasting. “Elevation” is the high-altitude farms behind some of the most distinctive coffees in the world.

SMALL, INDEPENDENT, AND PROUD OF IT

Small by design. Personal by choice.

Ember Elevation isn’t backed by a large roaster or a corporate team. It’s me, a trusted roasting partner, and a short list of coffees I genuinely believe in.

That means I see every order, because I pack every one myself. And every one genuinely helps a new Canadian business grow. Thank you for being part of that.
