ABOUT

Ember Elevation was created with a simple purpose — to offer exceptional whole-bean coffee sourced from high-elevation farms around the world.

Coffee grown at higher altitudes develops greater complexity, clarity, and character. These conditions produce beans with deeper sweetness, brighter acidity, and more distinctive flavor profiles.

Each coffee we offer is selected for balance, quality, and individuality. Rather than overwhelming the lineup with dozens of options, Ember Elevation focuses on a small, curated collection of coffees that represent some of the most respected growing regions in the world.

From the bright citrus and berry notes of Ethiopia to the smooth chocolate tones of Guatemala and the rich depth of Papua New Guinea, each origin brings its own unique expression to the cup.

Ember Elevation is built around the idea that great coffee should be simple, intentional, and memorable.
