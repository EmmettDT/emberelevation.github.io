OUR STORY / TORONTO, CANADA

It started with
one unforgettable cup.

Hi, I’m Emmett. I’m the person behind Ember Elevation, a small, independent coffee company in Toronto. This is the story of how one cup of coffee became a hobby, then an obsession, and now a business — and why every bag is still packed by my own hands.

Read my story ↓Explore the coffees ↗
THE STORY

A hobby I couldn’t put down.

The cup

For a long time, coffee was just coffee to me. Something hot in the morning. Nothing more.

Then, at a small specialty café, I had a cup that stopped me. It was sweet, bright, almost fruity — nothing like anything I’d tasted before. I remember thinking: how can coffee taste like this?

The obsession

I couldn’t let that question go. Not long after, I brought home my first espresso machine, a Breville Bambino Plus, determined to find out.

One shot at a time, I learned to dial in espresso. I practised latte art. I discovered how origin, processing, roasting and the conditions a coffee is grown in can completely change what you taste in the cup.

Today my setup is built around a La Marzocco Linea Micra, and most of my free time still goes into flat whites, experimenting, and searching for coffees where you can genuinely taste where they came from.

The leap

Somewhere along the way, I realized drinking great coffee wasn’t enough.

I wanted other people to taste what I’d found.

My family encouraged me to take the leap. So I did. Ember Elevation is a hobby I love, turned into a way to share it with you.

Why “Ember Elevation”? “Ember” is the heat and craft behind roasting. “Elevation” is the high-altitude farms behind some of the most distinctive coffees in the world.

SMALL, INDEPENDENT, AND PROUD OF IT

I can’t compete on size. So I compete on care.

Ember Elevation isn’t a big roaster with a marketing department and a warehouse. It’s one person who fell in love with coffee, a roasting partner I trust, and a short list of coffees I genuinely believe in.

That means when you order, a real person sees it, packs it by hand, and sends it to you. Every single order helps a new Canadian business take its next step.

FROM ORIGIN TO YOUR DOOR

How a bag reaches you.

  1. 01

    Chosen

    I select each coffee together with my roasting partner — only the ones I genuinely believe are worth drinking.

  2. 02

    Roasted

    Roasted by experienced craft roasters to reveal what’s already there. The coffee leads, not the roast.

  3. 03

    Packed by hand

    Every bag is packed by me, personally. No warehouse, no assembly line.

  4. 04

    Delivered

    Shipped to your door, with complimentary shipping across Canada on orders of $70 or more.

Elevation

Sourcing

Roasting
