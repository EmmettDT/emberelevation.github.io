CONTACT

Ember Elevation is built around a simple idea — to share exceptional high-elevation coffees from some of the world’s most respected growing regions. Each origin is selected for clarity, balance, and character in the cup.

Our coffees are sourced through specialty producers and roasted by a specialty roasting partner who helps bring out the unique qualities of each origin.

If you have questions about our coffees, brewing methods, or potential collaborations, we would be glad to hear from you.

COLLABORATIONS

Ember Elevation is always open to thoughtful collaborations with creators, cafés, and coffee enthusiasts who share a passion for specialty coffee culture. If you are interested in working together, feel free to reach out.
