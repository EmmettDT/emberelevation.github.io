THE COFFEE

HIGH-ELEVATION ORIGINS

Altitude plays a major role in the development of coffee flavor. Beans grown at higher elevations mature more slowly, allowing them to develop greater complexity and density.

SPECIALTY GRADE COFFEE

All Ember Elevation coffees are sourced from specialty-grade producers. Specialty coffee is evaluated for clarity, balance, sweetness, and overall cup quality.

ROAST PHILOSOPHY

Each origin is roasted to highlight its natural character. Lighter roasts preserve bright fruit and floral notes, while medium and medium-dark roasts develop deeper chocolate and caramel flavors.

BREWING

Whole-bean coffee preserves freshness and allows you to grind for your preferred brewing method. Whether prepared as espresso, pour-over, or French press, each coffee reveals a different expression in the cup.
